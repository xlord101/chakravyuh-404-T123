## ⚡ Overview
**Chakravyuh 2026** is the premier National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering. Featuring over **12+ national competitions**, hands-on engineering masterclasses, a **24-Hour AI Vibe Hackathon**, and a **₹1,00,000+ prize pool**, Chakravyuh bridges theoretical silicon architecture with live physical mastery.

Crafted with a **retro-tactile engineering aesthetic** inspired by **[ditto.site](https://www.ditto.site/)**, the platform merges crisp sage-mint paper surfaces, deep CRT oscilloscope canvases, JetBrains Mono telemetry specs, and Press Start 2P pixel hardware tags.

## 🚀 Key Modules & Architecture

### 1. 3D Exploded Hardware Canvas Experience
- **240 High-Resolution Disassembly Frames** pre-buffered into an HTML5 Canvas.
- **Scroll-Driven Scrubbing:** Synchronized using **Lenis** smooth scrolling and **Framer Motion** values.
- **Dynamic Telemetry HUD:** Real-time frame indicators, RF transceiver frequencies (2.4–5.8 GHz), 16-bit Sigma-Delta ADC readouts, and core VDD bus telemetry.
- **CRT Oscilloscope Scanlines:** Tactile phosphor aesthetic designed for hardware engineering.

### 2. Flagship Competition Matrix & Interactive Catalog
- **Robo-Kshethra: Autonomous Arena:** High-speed line followers and dynamic obstacle maze traversal (₹25,000 Prize Pool).
- **Silicon Matrix: Circuit Debugging:** Digital storage oscilloscopes, component forensics, and breadboard speedruns (₹18,000 Prize Pool).
- **AI Vibe Coding: 24h Hackathon:** Building intelligent IoT tools, embedded telemetry dashboards, and the **SparkPath** learning platform (₹30,000 Prize Pool).
- **Electro-Spectrum: Paper & Project Expo:** IEEE indexed research conference across 5G/6G, VLSI, and biomedical telemetry (₹20,000 Prize Pool).
- **Web-App Pulse:** Rapid prototyping of high-performance frontend interfaces for engineering instrumentation (₹15,000 Prize Pool).
- **Glitch Arena & Escape The Maze:** Laser tripwires, telegraph ciphers, and LAN tournaments (₹12,000 Prize Pool).
- **Interactive Rulebooks & Rounds Modal:** Detailed breakdown of qualifying rounds, scoring criteria, and faculty/student coordinator contacts.

### 3. 48-Hour Operations Timeline & Schedule
- Tabbed switcher for **Day 1 (Oct 29)** and **Day 2 (Oct 30)**.
- Filterable tracks: *Keynote, Robotics, Circuits, Coding, Paper, Non-Tech*.
- Venue locations mapped to specific campus halls (ADL-204, Robotics Arena, Innovation Hub).

### 4. Delegate Pass & Instant Registration Portal
- **Four Pass Tiers:**
  - *Bronze Explorer (₹150)*: Talks, project expo & welcome kit.
  - *Silver Competitor (₹350)*: Core tech event entry + Edge AI Masterclass + breadboard kit (Most Popular).
  - *Gold Hacker Squad (₹600)*: Full 4-member hackathon / robotics team entry with workstation & meals.
  - *All-Access VIP Delegate (₹999)*: Unlimited event access, VIP dinners, and official embroidered hoodie.
- **Dynamic Hardware Add-ons:** Breadboard IoT kit (+₹250), Official Hoodie (+₹450), 2-Day Buffet Lunch (+₹150).
- **Real-Time Total Calculation:** Instant fee computation with team squad inputs.
- **Instant Digital Pass Generator:** Dynamic SVG QR Code, unique delegate serial ID (`CKR26-ENTC-XXXX`), verified hologram watermark, and printable delegate badge with confetti animation.

### 5. E&TC Department Legacy & SparkPath Innovation
- Department research statistics: 35+ years of excellence, ₹2.5 Cr+ research funding, 18+ patents.
- Laboratory showcase: RF Microwave & Antenna Lab, VLSI Design Suite, Autonomous Robotics Sandbox, DSP Lab.
- **SparkPath Spotlight:** The gamified ENTC learning platform initiative (*Learn, Build, Break, Fix*).

### 6. Campus Venue, Transit Guide & Blueprints
- Commute directions via **Air (PNQ Airport)**, **Rail (Pune Junction)**, **Metro (Purple Line)**, and **Road**.
- Interactive blueprint zones (Zone A Auditorium, Zone B Arena, Zone C Labs, Zone D Computing).
